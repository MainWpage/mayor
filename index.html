Mayor's Corner

Leadership • Service • Progress

Hon. Victor R. Condez

Welcome to my official website.


As your public servant, I remain steadfast in my commitment to transparent governance, sustainable development, and responsive public service. Guided by integrity, accountability, and a genuine dedication to the welfare of our people, I continue to work together toward a stronger, more progressive, and more inclusive Bugasong.


Through infrastructure modernization, quality education, accessible healthcare, economic empowerment, environmental stewardship, and disaster resilience, our administration strives to create meaningful opportunities and lasting improvements that benefit every Bugasongnon.


I believe that good governance is not measured solely by completed projects, but by the positive impact these initiatives bring to the lives of our citizens. Together, let us build a future where every family can thrive, every community can prosper, and every citizen can take pride in a Bugasong that is resilient, innovative, and prepared for generations to come.

Latest News & Events

Pagyakap sa Kabarangayan: PuroKalusugan

Closing the month of July with unwavering commitment, the Bugasong Primary Care Facility carried on its mission of delivering accessible and people-centered healthcare through the PagYakap sa Kabarangayan: PuroKalusugan outreach in Barangay Sabang West. Despite the challenges brought by the southwest monsoon, health services continued uninterrupted under the leadership of Dr. Donelle Camiña, Doctor to the Barrio (DTTB), at the Barangay Covered Gym.
