Pagyakap sa Kabarangayan: PuroKalusugan

Closing the month of July with unwavering commitment, the Bugasong Primary Care Facility carried on its mission of delivering accessible and people-centered healthcare through the PagYakap sa Kabarangayan: PuroKalusugan outreach in Barangay Sabang West. Despite the challenges brought by the southwest monsoon, health services continued uninterrupted under the leadership of Dr. Donelle Camiña, Doctor to the Barrio (DTTB), at the Barangay Covered Gym.

#LARAGWAY:

Nagpatigayon kang food redemption ang Walang Gutom Program (WGP) sa banwa kang Bugasong kadiya nga Huwebes, Hulyo 23, sa Bugasong Public Market Gymnasium, sa pagpanguna kang opisina kang Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer (MSWDO) Lady Love B. Requintina, kaimaw man kang representante kang Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) R6, bilang kabahin kang anda regular nga aktibidades.

The meeting served as a venue to strengthen collaboration between the municipal government, barangay officials, and the DILG in promoting effective local governance and ensuring the efficient implementation of government programs and policies..
